Delete Your Data

For the Potato Galaxy games Puppy Sudoku and Smart Puppies.

How to ask

You do not have an account in our games. Your progress is saved online under a random player ID that the game creates, so we need that ID to find your data. The game fills it in for you:

  1. Open the game on the device you play on.
  2. Open Settings and tap Send feedback. This opens an email to us in your mail app.
  3. Write "Delete my data" above the lines the game has added, and send the email. Leave those lines in: one of them is your player ID.

If the email has no line that starts with "Player ID", update the game to its latest version and try again. You can also write to potato-galaxy@protonmail.com directly, and we will help you find the ID.

We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by replying to your email.

What we delete

  • The online copy of your game progress, such as your coins and gems, your level, the items on your board, your puppies and the names you gave them, and what you bought in the game.
  • The random player ID that copy was stored under.
  • Your email to us, once the request has been handled.

What is not deleted by this request

  • The progress on your device. It stays until you delete the game or clear its data in your device's settings. If you keep playing after your request, the game saves a new online copy; delete the game as well if you do not want that.
  • Analytics and advertising data held by Google (Firebase and AdMob). It is not stored under your name or your player ID, so we cannot pick out yours. Google deletes it or makes it anonymous after its own retention period, at most 14 months for analytics. You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in the device settings; our privacy policy explains how.
  • Purchase records kept by Google Play or the App Store. These are held by the store, not by us.

See the privacy policy for everything our games collect and why.
