Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who we are

Potato Galaxy publishes mobile games and runs this website. This policy covers the game Potato Galaxy Sudoku on iPhone and Android, and the website potatogalaxy.com. Contact: potato-galaxy@protonmail.com.

What data the app collects

The app never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location, and you do not create an account. It does collect the following:

  • Game progress. Your coins, the number of puzzles you have solved, your puppies, your difficulty level, and your daily reward streak are stored on your device. A copy is stored online so that your progress can be restored. The copy is kept under a random ID that the app creates for you; it is not linked to your name or email address.
  • Gameplay analytics. The app records events such as a puzzle being completed, the length of a play session, a daily reward being claimed, a puppy being unlocked, the ad removal screen being opened, and whether notifications were allowed. Together with these events it sends an app instance ID, your device model, operating system version, language, app version, and your approximate region (derived from your IP address). We use this to see how the game is played and to improve it.
  • Advertising data. The app shows ads. To deliver and measure them, the advertising service receives your IP address, device information, and how you interact with ads. It may also receive your device's advertising ID and use it to personalise ads, where you have allowed this.
  • Purchases. You can buy ad removal. The payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We learn only whether the purchase was made; we never see your card or billing details.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Ads and your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before it shows personalised ads. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

On any device you can limit ad personalisation in the system settings. On Android, go to Settings, then Google, then Ads, and delete or reset your advertising ID. On iPhone, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

Buying ad removal turns off the ads in the app.

Third-party services

The app uses these services, which process the data described above on our behalf or as independent providers:

  • Google AdMob for advertising, and the Google User Messaging Platform for the consent form. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase for gameplay analytics, and Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for the online copy of your progress. See privacy and security in Firebase.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for purchases. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell your data. These providers may store and process data in countries other than your own.

How long data is kept

Progress on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. The online copy of your progress is kept until you ask us to delete it. Analytics data is kept by Google for a limited period and then deleted automatically.

Children

Our apps are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country where that age is higher (up to 16 in parts of Europe). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you want their data removed, email us at the address above.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, or delete the data held about you, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

To ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted, email potato-galaxy@protonmail.com. Because your progress is stored under a random ID and not under your name, we may ask you for details from the app so that we can find your save. We answer within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

When the policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
