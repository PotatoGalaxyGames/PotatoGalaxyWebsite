Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Potato Galaxy ("we", "us") publishes mobile games and runs the website potatogalaxy.com. We are the controller of the personal data described here. This policy covers our games, including Potato Galaxy Sudoku on iPhone and Android, and this website. Together we call them the "services".

Contact us about anything in this policy at potato-galaxy@protonmail.com.

By using the services you also agree to our terms of service.

2. The data we collect

Our games never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location, and you do not create an account.

Data you give us

  • Messages to us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

Data collected automatically when you play

  • Game progress. Your coins, the number of puzzles you have solved, your puppies, your difficulty level, and your daily reward streak. This is stored on your device, and a copy is stored online so that your progress can be restored. The copy is kept under a random ID that the game creates for you; it is not linked to your name or email address.
  • Gameplay and device data. Events such as a puzzle being completed, the length of a play session, a daily reward being claimed, a puppy being unlocked, the ad removal screen being opened, and whether notifications were allowed. With these events come an app instance ID, your device model, operating system version, language, app version, and your approximate region (derived from your IP address).
  • Advertising data. Your IP address, device information, which ads you were shown, and how you interacted with them. Where you have allowed it, this includes your device's advertising ID.

Data we receive from others

  • Purchase confirmation. When you buy ad removal, the App Store or Google Play handles the payment and tells the game that the purchase was made. We never see your card or billing details.

Data that stays on your device

  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device. No notification data is sent to us.
  • The puzzle in progress and your settings.

3. Why we use your data

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), ours are the following:

  • To run the game for you (performance of our contract with you): saving and restoring your progress, delivering purchases, and answering your messages.
  • To improve the game and keep it working (our legitimate interests): understanding how the game is played, balancing difficulty, finding and fixing problems, and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • To show ads: non-personalised ads on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding a free game; personalised ads only with your consent, where consent is required.
  • To comply with the law (legal obligation): for example keeping records we are required to keep, or responding to lawful requests from authorities.

4. Ads and your choices

The games are free and funded by ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before it shows personalised ads. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

On any device you can limit ad personalisation in the system settings. On Android, go to Settings, then Google, then Ads, and delete or reset your advertising ID. On iPhone, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

Buying ad removal turns off the ads in that game.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data for money. We share data only as follows:

  • Google AdMob for advertising, and the Google User Messaging Platform for the consent form. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase for gameplay analytics, and Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for the online copy of your progress. See privacy and security in Firebase.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for purchases. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.
  • Authorities and legal claims. We may disclose data where the law requires it, or where it is needed to protect our rights or the safety of players.
  • A new owner. If Potato Galaxy or one of its games is sold or merged, the data moves to the new owner, who must keep following this policy or tell you about any change.

6. Transfers to other countries

Our service providers operate worldwide, so your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the transfer relies on safeguards recognised by law, such as an adequacy decision or the standard contractual clauses our providers offer.

7. How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Online copy of your progress: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics and advertising data: kept by Google for a limited period under its own retention rules, then deleted or made anonymous.
  • Emails you send us: for as long as needed to deal with your request, and afterwards only where we need a record.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the online copy of your progress can be read and written only by the game installation that created it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that data will never be accessed without permission.

9. Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country where that age is higher (up to 16 in parts of Europe). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used a game and you want their data removed, email us at the address above.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights:

  • to see the data held about you and receive a copy of it;
  • to have it corrected or deleted;
  • to object to its use, or to have its use restricted;
  • to withdraw a consent you have given, at any time;
  • to complain to your local data protection authority.

United States. Some state laws, such as California's, give residents the right to know, correct, and delete their data, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of data for targeted advertising. We do not sell data for money. Showing personalised ads may count as "sharing" under those laws; you can opt out with the device settings described in section 4. We do not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

To use your rights, email potato-galaxy@protonmail.com. Because your progress is stored under a random ID and not under your name, we may ask you for details from the game so that we can find your data. We answer within 30 days.

11. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy

When the policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we also tell you in the game or on this website.
